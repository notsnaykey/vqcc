## 3. Public frontier per benchmark: design and lineage

### 3.1 invsqrt (fusion): 21.21× at 62 qubits

**Architecture (unchanged since Sumit Chongder, 23 Oct; refined by ~17 public steps).** Gluzdov's description: binary shift stages normalise x to a mantissa M ∈ [1,2); their flags give the shift, e its parity, s half of it: `1/√x = 2^(s−10)·y`, `y = 2^((e−1)/2)/√M ∈ (0.5,1]`. An 8-bit lookup addressed by (e, 7 high mantissa bits) loads the coefficients of a centred quadratic; the low bits form an offset whose digits are all ±1: `δ = Σ(2d_i − 1)2^(i−22)`, `R = K₁ − C₂δ`, `y = K₀ − Rδ`. Because every digit is ±1, each Horner row is an uncontrolled Gidney add/subtract wrapped in CNOT flips (`Y − A = ~(~Y + A)`) with rounding through the carry-in: no product temporaries. The derivative register is split into a short residual and coarse bits (Rudra), the constant high part of the result is phased early (static-high checkpoint), the curvature is looked up per group of segments and erased by measurement, the lowest accumulator bit is virtual (zongaobian), the output phase is fused (shifts by 1 and 2 by controlled swaps, each of the 41 calibrated rotations reads its bit through one temporary — marsyas). Coefficients use short precisions fitted on the reachable inputs (Kozykorpesh's `Cfg(8,19,27,1,(5,))`, 256 `[K2,K1,K0]` rows); rotation angles are LP-calibrated over the whole domain (Mizero/lightningv08); worst phase error 0.99932 of tolerance at x = 161, so the ε term "sits on its floor" (each rotation 28.77 T).

**Width reduction** came almost entirely from storage/uncomputation: 103 q (Sumit) → 95 (Rudra) → 92 (anazemcev, marsyas) → 84 (Gluzdov) → 82 (Phuc Thai) → 78 (busyaprime) → 66 → **63 (mrmeows, 25–27 Oct)** → **62 (yoyobar, 30 Oct)**. mrmeows: lookup outputs that are Boolean functions of other outputs are recomputed where used, carry prefixes finished and erased early, increment-tail workspaces removed by temporarily erasing the top accumulator bit, input bits and normalization flags parked in freed slots; 23 Toffoli uncomputes turned into CNOTs by history caches. yoyobar: products go straight into the output-word wires (5 live temporaries instead of 32), fresh helpers replaced by in-place XORs and by classical logic on measurement bits. Several authors measured that 61 is not reachable (Q 62 is hit in eight windows; one qubit ≈ 26 Toffoli-eq; Q 60 would cost 32–57).

### 3.2 dqi (combinatorial optimisation): 65.11× at 891 qubits

**Architecture.** 70 twelve-bit syndromes S_i = Σ_j e_j α^((i+1)j) over GF(2¹²) on 840 qubits, ≤35 errors among 4,095 positions, 4,095 classical mask words M_j; the circuit multiplies the phase by (−1)^Σ|e_j ∧ M_j|, returns the syndromes and leaves everything else clean. All public decoders since 26 Oct descend from **jday96314's continued-fraction Schur decoder** (GitHub `QuantumAI`, round 6 `initial_experiments/6/agent14`, no licence stated): the syndrome generating function is read at 17 nodes in GF(2²⁴) (the degree-2 extension), **jets** (steps that divide outright, paying a field inversion) for the early nodes and **homogeneous chains** carrying (N : D) for the rest; continued-fraction synthesis `(N,B) ← (l_i N + q_i B, N)`; amplitude polynomial `C = A/B′ mod B` so the Forney-style scan costs 11 Toffoli per point (zero ladder) with CZ-only phase kickback; the syndrome words are transformed in place so the decoder needs only ~50 qubits beyond the 840-qubit bank. SomeJoint's additions: **confluent Hermite extraction** (G and G′ at 17 double nodes instead of G at 35 nodes; jet inversion `J(u,v) = (u⁻¹, v·u⁻²)`), inversion through the norm `N = u·ū ∈ GF(2¹²)` descending a tower to GF(64) with cached quadratic corrections, GPT-6 Astra Pro's 658-Toffoli jet, streamed conversion ("aligned conversion route with measured low heads, streamed full-head fusion with a monic root, replay erasure"), the two optimised halves joined, the **narrow return** (each of nine homogeneous extraction nodes keeps its terminal value while undoing its chain, retiring the numerator in two halves; −15,862 T for +11 q), and **carried pole flags** (ahoirg's idea: only group 6's flag stored, groups 7–16 rebuilt through a carry bank; 898→892). Width moves: Alex Chilton 892→883 (§2), heliosli's pole-flag release 897→889, manwithacat 889→888, Özer 877 (fused decoder), hengck23's projective extraction (889 with 48 private qubits, proved optimal for the op set), syed3000's **C1 decoder** (retained-head chain, block-Wronskian telescoped prefix jump, narrow return, flag carry, MBU of recompute steps, Khattar–Gidney conditionally clean AND ladders, exact product caching): **902 → 891**. Toffoli moves on the same family: measurement-based clearing of flags (manwithacat HW-06/07/08/09; busyaprime's 24 sites), **exact product caching** (busyaprime: −2,803 T/shot; Özer's 576 history caches; yoyobar's lead-product reuse), one-CCZ rewrites (yoyobar). Failure accounting: every decoder in the family keeps approximate rare branches (chance zeros into inversions, third gaps, flag-free folds k ≥ 17), priced by R(ε⁺); the frontier files draw 0 errors on most salts but not all (Alex's v85 lost 0.037 on the board to one error), hence the 48-draw salt sweeps.

### 3.3 dfthcsos (chemistry): 10.74× at 428 qubits

**Architecture.** Factorization (R,B,C) = **(9,143,39)** (syed3000's table, from Lera's compact-groups pipeline; the public frontier used Lera's (9,143,40) from 1–3 Nov and SomeJoint's (8,143,60) full-XOR table before that), **8-bit words** (brot 8), **J = 5 words per loader, 15 loaders** (lightningv08), 76 + 9·143 = **1,363 addresses** (11 address qubits), each loader ≤ 128 qubits (11 address + 40 word + ≤77 scratch). The cost model: Q = 125 persistent + 303 inner-state-preparation workspace = 428 (the loaders' own scratch is below that maximum, so making loaders narrower saves nothing; the 428 figure needs B ≥ 129 at 8 bits — the QROAM "cliff" Rustad found; 9-bit words push Q to ~736, 7-bit words would halve the workspace but no 7-bit table has passed 20 mHa). T = 15,525 = two loader passes 8,872 (2 × 4,436 ANDs) + inner prep 4,086 + Givens 2,250 + outer 317. λ_eff 47.0175 sits 0.5 % above its floor 46.786 at the fixed shifts.

**How the table is made (Lera, then syed3000):** Cholesky leaves clustered by orbital (compactness), OMP vector selection, long float64 L-BFGS + Adam at the shape, rows dealt across 16-address blocks, progressive rounding with coded bit 0 (in 11 of 15 tables bit 0 of each word is an affine function of the four low address bits on every 16-address block — a first-order Reed–Muller codeword — so it costs almost nothing to load; Lera leaves the pattern open until the frozen angles pin it; syed3000 uses Babai nearest-plane rounding with lazy code commitment and a sign-mixing penalty), then an augmented-Lagrangian refit of W, H2_I holding error₂ and E_gap on their limits (phucthaiv02's idea; manwithacat's `dfthc_refit`; SomeJoint's guarded tangent steps + exact Jacobi column gauge). Error budget at the frontier: error₂ 19.999998 mHa, error₁ 10.6 mHa, E_gap 3.9148990 (floor 3.914898).

**How the loaders are compiled (mrmeows → Özer → lightningv08 → Lera → matousakura/manwithacat → syed3000):** loader k writes the XOR of batch k's five words with batch k−1's as a function of the 11-bit address, expanded in ANF over 4 low and 7 high address bits; every low and high monomial is cached; mixed terms are covered **two per Toffoli** by the GF(2) identity `(h₁+l₂)(h₂+l₁) = h₁l₁ + h₂l₂ + h₁h₂ + l₁l₂` with low-only/high-only terms corrected from the caches; **kernel relations** (GF(2) relations among products that the table does not need) let two mixed terms share one Toffoli when the relations can be paired (matroid parity; admission test by full rank of the alternating pairing matrix); the four-slot low cache holds ≤4 composite low monomials created on demand and released by reset (0 Toffoli), hence `T_k = 89 + products` and `rel_k = 525 − 2(T_k − 89)`; the redundant sign bit (bit 7 = bit 6) of each word is borrowed as scratch (heliosli, avazaku); work registers are erased by measurement with classically controlled phase repair (the loaders are exact only on the reachable signed-gauge domain, per the host ruling in discussion 753092). Progress on the same words: Lera's emitter 316–321 T (uncoded) / 298–310 (coded) → HW-12/13/14 314 / 297–296 → syed3000 306 / 292 (hub-free grouping of low monomials, exact matroid-parity pairs, don't-care absorption, a fifth low-cache slot inside the cap, evolutionary planner search scored by the exact verifier): **4,436 ANDs per pass**.

## 4. What the top private teams likely do beyond the public frontier — [INFERENCE]

Facts to reason from: the public plateau is ~24.53–24.57 and copied within hours; rank 10 has been ×1.068–1.084 above it every day since 10-29 (now 26.358, i.e. ×1.238 on the product of ratios); rank 1 is 28.832 (×1.174 on the score, ×1.62 on the product); the SomeJoint team's own board score (26.03) is ×1.061 above its published zip; malachiwmoody's 31-Oct optimistic ceilings for the *known* designs (invsqrt 24.5×, dqi 72.5×, chemistry 11.8×) multiply to 27.55 < 28.83, so the leader holds at least one architecture not in any public notebook; Breaking1800 reached 27.996 on 11-02 before SomeJoint's 892q line was public.

1. **DQI is where the biggest private gap almost certainly is**, because it is where the public line itself moved most (53× → 65× in a week), where a 6 % gain costs "only" −24k Toffoli or −27 qubits, and where every public author lists unclaimed leads: (a) a cheaper or in-place GF(2²⁴)/GF(2¹²) multiplication (rank < 99 / < 42 in a tower basis; ≈ −1.4k T per unit of rank; an in-place 24×24 multiplier keeping its factor at ≈ 99 T with ≤ 18 scratch would reopen the width route to 885–887 ≈ −1 % each); (b) a conversion/synthesis route with fewer inversions (hengck23 priced "scan + extraction + synthesis alone" at 302k T, so a decoder near 320k T is conceivable only by removing most of the conversion and reverse premium — a projective/fraction-free Euclid or Hankel route that the public teams costed as worse but a private team may have made work); (c) the quadratic-map norm decomposition (≈ −2k T); (d) fewer, better-placed flags with a lower priced failure rate. SomeJoint's own ×1.061 gap over its public zip is probably mostly here (their datasets `qcc-dqi-898`, `qcc-dqi-900-undo`, `qcc-dqi-892-q26` show they publish each round a few days late, and the C1/"ladder" kits were built with GPT-6 Astra agents that keep iterating).
2. **Chemistry: a new table shape or a 7-bit table.** The cost model's known discontinuities are the levers: (i) a passing **7-bit** table would halve the inner-preparation workspace (Q 428 → ~270) and cut Givens Toffolis, worth ×1.5 on the task (≈ +15 % on the score) — every public attempt failed the gate, but Özer's twins and Lera's converged schedules were each worth 2–3 mHa of noise², so a large GPU budget could plausibly close the remaining ~3–5 mHa; (ii) **fewer rows**: R=8 at C=45 is already public at 10.41× (Lera) and R=4 "if it works" is worth +21 % on chemistry (malachiwmoody's chem-K1R); a team with Lera-class training compute could hold a (8,143,·) or (4,·,·) table with a syed3000-class compiler at ~11–12×; (iii) a loader compiler below 4,436 ANDs per pass (the public line went 4,746 → 4,436 in three days; the two-level floor malachiwmoody quotes for the older table was 3,715 vs 4,183 shipped, so ~10 % more is plausible). λ_eff is exhausted (0.5 % above its floor).
3. **invsqrt: a different arithmetic, not more gate patches.** The public line is within a few percent of its stage floors and every width cut is priced as a loss, so a private 22–24× almost certainly comes from (a) mrmeows' uncompiled **constant-seed / degree-3 seed** model (fewer Horner rows at the price of a bigger lookup; passes the domain at 0.617 of tolerance, leaving margin to drop rotations), (b) fewer rotations (41 × 7.2 Toffoli-eq is 18.6 % of the cost; a design that phases fewer output bits or uses the margin to drop the smallest rotations), or (c) a 60–61 qubit layout that reorganises the eight full-width windows at once (rows priced at ≤ 26 Toffoli-eq per qubit).
4. **Portfolio hygiene the leaders certainly do:** salt sweeps to pick decoders by expected R rather than one draw (one failure = −0.15 % score), re-rolling hashes is possible but a lucky public draw does not transfer to the private salt; the leaders' private circuits are presumably exact where the public family is approximate (the public C1/Schur family fails 237–253 of 429 adversarial inputs).
5. **Arithmetic check of what rank 1 needs** (×1.62 on the product): e.g. invsqrt 24× (×1.13), dqi 85× (×1.31), chemistry 12.5× (×1.16) multiply to 1.70 — i.e. a new DQI conversion/multiplier *and* either a 7-bit chemistry table or an R ≤ 8 table *and* a seed-level invsqrt change; no single task can do it alone (dqi alone would need ~105×, T ≈ 245k at 891 q).
